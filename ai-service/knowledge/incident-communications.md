# Customer incident communications

## First update
State what customers are experiencing, when the impact began if known, which products or regions are affected, and that investigation is active. Use confirmed facts only. Do not speculate about a root cause, attribute blame, or expose internal hostnames, credentials, personal data, or security-sensitive details.

## Ongoing updates
Publish updates at the incident's agreed communication interval even when there is no material change. Separate confirmed observations from actions in progress, identify available workarounds and their risks, and give the next update time instead of an unsupported recovery estimate. Keep public, customer-specific, and internal engineering channels distinct.

## Resolution update
Confirm that service indicators and customer checks have recovered before announcing resolution. Summarize the observed impact, recovery time, and any safe workaround rollback. Track root-cause analysis and preventive actions separately; do not claim that an investigation is complete before the incident review is approved.
